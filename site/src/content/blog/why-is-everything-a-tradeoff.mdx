description: "Pick any two good things and you usually cannot have both at full strength. Speed or safety. Storage density or fast access. Depth or breadth. The pattern is so common it starts to feel like a law of nature rather than a coincidence. This is an attempt to work out why. Is a tradeoff a feature of the world itself, or just a sign that you have already taken the easy wins and reached the edge of what is possible? And if it is the latter, can you move that edge, or get both after all?"
